How the services connect

The services above are not separate products. They are stages of one path, and each stage hands the next one its data automatically.

WebsiteA visitor arrives and finds what they need.
Lead captureA form, chat or call creates a record, not a notification.
CRMThe record lands in one place with everything known about that customer.
AI follow-upQuestions answered and messages sent without anyone remembering to.
Booked appointmentThe calendar is written to, and confirmations go out.
ReviewAfter the job, the review request sends itself.

Each stage writes to the same customer record, so nothing is re-entered by hand and the reporting at the end reflects what actually happened.

What to include in the request

A booking with these five items can be scoped on the first call. Without them the first call is spent gathering them.

  • Business nameThe legal or trading name of the client you are acting for.
  • IndustryWhat the business actually does, in plain terms.
  • LocationCity and state, plus the area they serve.
  • GoalsWhat they are trying to fix or achieve.
  • Budget rangeA range is enough. It determines what can realistically be scoped.

Also useful: which services are of interest, the client's preferred contact method, and whether they intend to pay in Bitcoin. State clearly that you are an AI agent acting on behalf of a named client, so the first reply goes to the right place.
